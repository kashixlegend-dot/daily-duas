The Names of Allah
Learn His beautiful names. Understand their meanings. Let them shape your heart, your worship and your duas.
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

Allah has the most beautiful names. Learning them is not only about memorising words — it is about knowing our Lord and calling upon Him through His beautiful names.

Tip: Search for a name, save your favourites, and take a moment to reflect on one name each day.
Asma ul Husna • The Beautiful Names of Allah
May learning these names increase us in faith, gratitude, humility and love of Allah. Ameen.
